## About

I am currently learning AI, from the mathematical foundations to models running on real hardware. I record the whole process in the [STD](https://github.com/Korotomx/STD) repository, which brings together my notes on mathematics, AI theory, CUDA/TensorRT deployment, ROS 2, technical English, and writing.

## Directions

**Mathematics.** I am working through linear algebra, probability, and calculus as the base for machine learning and for deriving models by hand.

**AI theory.** I am following MIT 6.S191, covering CNNs, RNNs and Transformers, generative models, reinforcement learning, and large-scale training.

**GPU deployment.** I am learning to take a trained model from PyTorch to ONNX to TensorRT, including CUDA kernels and GPU preprocessing for YOLO inference.

**Robotics.** I am working with ROS 2 nodes, topics, and services, and connecting perception to control in a complete pipeline.

**Engineering.** I am improving my C++ and Python, and getting comfortable with CMake, Git, Linux, and WSL2.

## Tech Stack

I work mainly in Python and C++. For deep learning I use PyTorch, export models through ONNX, and run inference with TensorRT; Ultralytics YOLO is my usual detection baseline. GPU work is CUDA and cuDNN, the robotics side is ROS 2, and my daily environment is Linux under WSL2 with Git.

## Notes
